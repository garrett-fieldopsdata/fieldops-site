FAQ

Honest answers.

How does the free trial work?
Sign up, add a card, and you get 7 days of full access — you're charged $0 until the trial ends. If it earns its keep, it rolls into $29/month flat. If not, cancel in two clicks before day 8 and pay nothing; your data stays exportable either way.
Is my data safe? Where is it stored?
Your data syncs to secure cloud storage (Cloudflare's global network) tied to your account. It's available on any device you log into and backed up automatically. You can also export a full local backup anytime from Settings.
Does it work on my phone?
Yes — it's designed to work on any device. Add it to your home screen (Safari: Share → Add to Home Screen, Chrome: Menu → Install app) and it runs like a native app. Works great on both iPhone and Android.
Does it actually work offline?
Yes. Open the app once with signal, and you can use it fully without internet — add jobs, create invoices, log time. Everything syncs automatically the moment you're back online.
How do online payments work?
When you send an invoice or estimate, your customer gets a private portal link. They can view, approve, and pay — no account needed on their end. Payment processing setup instructions are in the app under Settings.
Can I use it for my specific trade?
Yes — the app works for any field service business. The onboarding lets you select your trade and pre-configures everything accordingly. HVAC, plumbing, electrical, landscaping, roofing, painting, cleaning, concrete — all covered.
What if I have a problem or want a feature?
Email garrett@fieldopsdata.com or use the feedback button inside the app. We reply personally and read every request. Beta users have direct input into what gets built next.
How is this different from Jobber or Housecall Pro?
Those are great tools — for bigger operations. They start at $50–$200/month and have features most small crews will never use. FieldOps Pro is built to be simple enough to set up in 10 minutes, use on a phone in the field, and priced so it's never a burden for a 1–5 person business.
